About this policy

Selah is a church presentation project maintained by Sami Reddy. This policy covers this website, its resource downloads, the current Selah desktop app, and information you choose to send us. It describes the features currently implemented; availability can vary by app version.

When you visit the website

You can browse and download resources without creating a Selah account. This website does not currently use advertising trackers, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or browser storage to remember your activity. Website fonts are served with the site. Resource searches and filters run in your browser and are not sent to a Selah search service.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Requests to hosting and download services also expose standard connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your work in the app

Songs, imported Bibles, programs, presentations, templates, notes, media, preferences, and recovery data are stored on your computer. The current app does not require a Selah account or provide cloud sync. This website does not receive your local app library.

Exports, portable program bundles, and full backups can contain your content and personal information, including notes and media. You decide where to save them and whom to share them with. Your operating system, backup provider, or anyone with access to your computer or exported files may be able to access that information.

Downloads and optional connections

  • Website resources and releases. App download and changelog pages request public release information from GitHub. External Bible files and app installers are downloaded from GitHub services when you choose them.
  • App downloads. Bible catalog browsing/import and update checks contact GitHub. Update checks include the app version. Online font previews and font downloads contact GitHub or Google Fonts; downloaded fonts are cached locally.
  • Luma. The optional assistant runs with a local model and requires you to enable it. Managed setup downloads model/runtime files from Hugging Face. The current integration processes prompts locally rather than sending them to a hosted AI service; prompts, proposals, and results are kept in memory during use.
  • Remote and service cues. If you start remote control, paired devices on your local network can receive presentation content and, for speaker views, private notes. The current remote service uses HTTP, so use a trusted network. It uses a session cookie for pairing, separate from this website. HTTP/OSC service cues send the commands you configure to your chosen destinations.

These services receive the connection information needed to deliver your request. Their own policies apply: GitHub, Google, and Hugging Face. They may process information in countries other than yours.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message, attachments, and other details you provide. GitHub issues and comments are public and may include your GitHub profile. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, improve Selah, and address privacy or security requests. Email is handled through Gmail under Google’s privacy policy.

Please share only what is needed. Remove private speaker notes, congregation details, passwords, pairing codes, and unrelated personal information from screenshots or logs.

Use and sharing of information

We use information you provide to deliver support, maintain and secure the project, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may share relevant information with the service providers used to handle your request, where required by law, or where necessary to address abuse or protect people’s safety. Public GitHub submissions are visible to others.

Storage, retention, and security

Local app data stays on your computer until you remove it or change its storage. Uninstalling the app may leave its data folder, exports, and backups behind. You control those copies.

We keep support correspondence while needed to handle the request and follow-up, address security issues, or meet legal requirements. We do not promise a fixed retention period for third-party hosting, email, or GitHub records; those providers control their own retention. You can ask us to delete correspondence we control, subject to applicable requirements. No storage or communication method can guarantee complete security.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without a Selah account, choose whether to use online app features, stop remote sessions, and keep or remove local data and exports. For access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have sent us, email samireddynani0@gmail.com. We may need information to verify the request. Available rights depend on the law that applies to you, and may include contacting your local data protection authority. For information held independently by GitHub or Google, use their privacy request channels.

Children’s information

This website is intended to explain a church presentation tool, not to collect information from children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate action. Please avoid including children’s information in public issues, support attachments, or shared program files.

Updates and contact

We will update this page when Selah’s data practices change and revise the date above. Review it when using new features. For privacy questions, contact Sami Reddy at samireddynani0@gmail.com or visit Contact us.
